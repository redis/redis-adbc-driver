TPC-H SF1 Q3, Q5, Q7, Q8 and Q9 fail under the recorded 60-second / 8 GiB client budget on v0.0.31: [#579](https://github.com/redis/redis-adbc-driver/issues/579) (Q3 timeout), [#581](https://github.com/redis/redis-adbc-driver/issues/581) (Q5 OOM), [#582](https://github.com/redis/redis-adbc-driver/issues/582) (Q7 OOM), [#583](https://github.com/redis/redis-adbc-driver/issues/583) (Q8 OOM) and [#584](https://github.com/redis/redis-adbc-driver/issues/584) (Q9 OOM). Their joins are snowflakes: dimensions join each other (customer through orders, nation through supplier or customer), so the existing streaming star join, which requires every equality to touch the fact, rejects them. The eager join then materializes every intermediate row as maps, and its greedy order can join two dimensions on a low-cardinality key (Q5 customers to suppliers by nation, Q7 suppliers to customers by nation) before the fact. A 10% subset of the SF1 data already needs 6–10 GB.

Stream the fact through pre-joined dimension branches with the existing exact join semantics, bounded memory and an eager fallback. Keep current pure-star plans and results unchanged. Q1/Q18 decimal aggregation, Q4 semi-join probing, Q21 decorrelation and the DuckDB-over-ADBC route (#599) are handled separately and are excluded here. SF1 timing remains a separate, coordinator-controlled verification on Redis Software with verified 4x QPF.
