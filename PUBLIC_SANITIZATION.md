# Public source snapshot

This version was migrated as a standalone root snapshot. Original commit history
was intentionally omitted. Driver source, workflows, and synthetic test fixtures
retain their original contents.

Private deployment control records, raw logs, archived operational output, and
profiling artifacts were omitted. Concrete deployment identifiers and addresses
in retained benchmark records were redacted or replaced with documentation-only
placeholders. Benchmark figures remain historical, labeled by their original
server distribution and settings; they are not new validation results.

Original unsanitized exports remain in private maintainer storage. Historical
release pages have no uploaded binaries; only v0.0.18 retains its original assets.
