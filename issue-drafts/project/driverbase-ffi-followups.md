## Risk

Regenerating `driver.go` from the pinned or current template would drop the handle holders and bring back the `checkptr` abort recorded in #458.

## Proposed work

1. Keep this issue open until adbc-drivers/driverbase-go#247 merges and we adopt a driverbase pseudo-version that contains it. Then regenerate and diff against `go/pkg/driver.go`, keeping only intended differences.
2. Offer upstream the GC-removal PR the maintainers outlined (steps a–c), or wait for one. Then remove the four `runtime.GC()` calls by regenerating, not by hand-editing.
3. Re-run the ownership and lifetime gates used for #446 and #458 (race + `checkptr=2` + `cgocheck2`, the native ASAN/UBSAN smoke, the CRUD close-latency diagnostic) and record before/after numbers.

- The lifetime gates pass.
